# Dataset: Synthetic Hyderabad Event Opportunities v1
Exactly 24 purpose-built, invented records, generated deterministically by `data/generate.py`. Snapshot date November14,2026. Not downloaded from Kaggle, Instagram or Kaivalyam's systems. No client names, phone numbers, messages or genuine transactions. No training/test split: this is a transparent rule engine, not a trained ML model. The fixture is a demonstration, not a representative sample or business baseline.

Fields: ID, INR currency, illustrative event type, sales stage, quote and event dates, quote price, estimated delivery cost, deposit due date/amount, deposits received, optional add-on selling price and delivery cost, existing add-on selection, vendor capacity, client opt-out and confirmed add-on budget. All monetary values are integer paise; one row = one quote version/opportunity, one candidate add-on, one cumulative scheduled deposit milestone. Vendor cost includes assumed delivery costs; business overheads and taxes are not modelled. No quoted amount is a real price from the company.

Event categories and add-ons are proposed hypotheses, not a verified complete service catalogue. Public Instagram descriptions informed the decor/celebration context only. No follower data used as a revenue measure. Some records intentionally illustrate weak margins or late deposits; these do not imply those conditions occur at Kaivalyam.

Real pilot needs consented exports from the cousin: anonymized inquiry IDs, dates, quoted/booked amounts, wins/losses, estimated AND actual vendor costs, paid invoices, deposit schedule and verified receipts, add-on outcomes, capacity and permission to contact. No need to share customer names or phone numbers for the first analysis. Exclude taxes/refunds consistently; reconcile to accounting records. Deduplicate revised quotes before analysis.
