Sahitya Reddy runs Kaivalyam Events in Hyderabad, and I wanted to see how ServiceNow could help a small events business like hers. 🌸

So I built a small app that spots quotes needing a follow-up, margins running too thin, overdue deposits and add-ons worth suggesting. It only gives advice; people still make the calls.

I tested it on 24 made-up events in a ServiceNow developer instance, and every result matched. ✅
